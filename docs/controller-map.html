PS5 DualSense → ESP32 RC Transmitter

Controller map · esp32-ps5-ppm

Flight controls Launch Trim Toggles (saved) Pairing & status
L2

Release launch

Elevator back to stick + trim. Moving the right stick ~40% also releases.

L1

Launch hold

Full up elevator for hand launch. Long strong rumble. Clears on disconnect.

LIGHT BAR

Reverse status

Colour shows which surfaces are reversed (see legend below).

PLAYER LEDs

Travel

1 LED = 100% throw  ·  all 5 = 125% throw.

LEFT STICK

Throttle

Centre = idle, push up = more power. Spring-back safe.

PS

Connect

First time: hold PS + Create until it flashes. After that, just PS.

R2

Trim elevator up

Hold to repeat. R1 + R2 together = reset trim. Saved.

R1

Trim elevator down

Tick per step · long buzz at centre · one-sided buzz at limit.

▲

Elevator reverse

Toggle elevator direction. Short rumble.

●

Travel 100 / 125%

Rudder + elevator 1000–2000 µs ↔ 875–2125 µs.

■

Rudder reverse

Toggle rudder direction. Short rumble.

RIGHT STICK

Elevator + Rudder

Up/down = elevator  ·  left/right = rudder.

Light bar

  • Green: all normal
  • Blue: rudder reversed
  • Yellow: elevator reversed  Red: both

PPM channels (AETR)

  • CH1 Rudder (3-ch receiver)
  • CH2 Elevator · CH3 Throttle
  • CH4 Rudder · CH5–8 centre

Outputs

  • GPIO25 900 MHz TX module
  • GPIO26 TX16S trainer jack
  • GPIO2 LED blinks while sticks move

If the PS5 disconnects

  • Module: throttle idle, surfaces centre
  • Trainer: stops, TX16S takes over
  • Launch hold cleared
